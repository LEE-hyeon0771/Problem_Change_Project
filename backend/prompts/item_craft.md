## ITEM-WRITING CRAFT (applies to every item type)

You are not "making a question about a passage". You are producing an item that must
survive review by a Korean exam committee. Everything below is what a reviewer checks.

### C1. Passage suitability — check BEFORE writing

Each type needs specific raw material. If the passage lacks it, do not force the item:
build the closest defensible variant and record what you compromised in `meta.note`.

Never invent content that is not in the passage to make a type fit.

### C2. Distractor construction — recipes, not labels

Build each distractor by applying ONE named transformation to the correct answer.
Naming the transformation is what keeps distractors principled instead of random.

| Transformation | Recipe |
|---|---|
| `scope_narrow` | Replace the key's general term with one specific instance the passage mentions. The result is true but covers only part of the passage. |
| `scope_broad` | Replace the key's specific claim with a generic life lesson. True in general, but not what this passage argues. |
| `polarity_flip` | Keep every content word; reverse the stance (support↔oppose, increase↔decrease, enable↔prevent). |
| `agent_swap` | Keep the action; change who performs or receives it (owners↔policymakers, market↔government). |
| `degree_shift` | Change quantification (always↔sometimes, all↔most, requires↔may help). |
| `causal_reverse` | Swap cause and effect, or turn a correlation into a cause. |
| `partial_truth` | Take a true statement from the passage that is a supporting detail, not the main point. |
| `keyword_lure` | Reuse the passage's most memorable words while asserting something the passage never claims. |

`partial_truth` and `keyword_lure` are the strongest distractors because they are
**verifiably present in the text**. Use them when the level calls for attractive options.

### C3. Option surface parity — reviewers reject items on these alone

- All options within ±30% of each other in word count. A visibly longer option reads as the answer.
- Same grammatical shape: all noun phrases, or all clauses, or all -ing phrases. Never mixed.
- Same level of specificity: if one option names a concrete entity, all should.
- No grammatical give-away: an article or auxiliary before the blank must fit every option
  (`a`/`an`, singular/plural agreement, to-infinitive vs bare).
- No option may be eliminable purely by grammar when the item is not a grammar item.
- Do not start every distractor with the same word while the key starts differently.

### C4. Answer uniqueness — prove it, do not assume it

Run this internally before output:

1. For EACH distractor, write one sentence naming the exact textual evidence that
   defeats it. If you cannot point at the passage, the distractor is not defeated —
   rewrite it.
2. Ask: could a well-prepared student defend any distractor as also correct?
   If yes, the item is broken. Tighten the key or the distractor, not the stem.
3. Ask: is the key defensible using ONLY the passage, with no outside knowledge?
   If it needs world knowledge, rewrite it.

### C5. Explanation quality (Korean, for the student)

A good 해설 cites evidence; a bad one asserts. Structure:

1. **근거 위치** — quote or point to the passage span that settles the answer.
2. **정답 근거** — why that span forces the key.
3. **대표 오답** — name ONE tempting distractor and the exact reason it fails,
   using the C2 transformation name in plain Korean
   (예: "범위가 너무 좁다", "인과가 뒤집혔다", "지문에 있는 말이지만 중심 내용이 아니다").

Forbidden: "문맥상 자연스럽다", "가장 적절하다" with no evidence, restating the option.

### C6. Known failure modes — a reviewer rejects these on sight

- Two defensible answers. The single most common rejection.
- A distractor that is *obviously* absurd — it wastes a slot and lowers difficulty.
- The key is the longest, most detailed, or most hedged option.
- The item can be solved without reading the passage (general knowledge suffices).
- The explanation repeats the option text instead of citing passage evidence.
- The passage was rewritten or paraphrased when the type required preserving it.
